# Pitch skeleton

A structure, not copy. The hiring manager writes every line, in their own words; the brackets say what each part has to do. Start from the phrases in `hire/role-brief.md`.

**[Title: what the designer would call this job]**

[The opening, about 40 to 80 words. What they'd work on, why it matters to *them*, and why now. Start from the strongest phrase in the role brief. The designer is the subject of most sentences.]

[Pay: the range, base and equity.]

[The work: the first project, in a few concrete lines. Name the real thing.]

[Who they'll work with every day, and what that's like.]

[What's hard about it. Honest, short.]

[The spike: the one or two things they must be exceptional at. Not a list of everything.]

[How to apply, and what happens next: the stages, and when they'll hear back. Fill this in after Stage 3, once the stages are set.]
