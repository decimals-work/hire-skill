# Work trial: [role] at [company]

Sent to the candidate before the trial. The hiring manager writes the lines in brackets in their own words.

**The role:** [title], [pay range]

**The problem:** [a real one from the product, near the spike, in two or three sentences.]

**What good looks like:** [what you'd be happy to see by the end. Not a finished design.]

**When:** [date and hours. At least one full day.]

**Where:** [address, or remote. Travel, if any, and who pays for it.]

**Pay:** [the amount (we recommend at least $1,000 per day, or the equivalent), how it's paid and when.]

**Who you'll work with:** [names and roles, and when each of them joins.]

**The day:**
- Kick-off: [time, who, what you'll cover]
- Check-in: [time, who]
- The open question: [the part with no straightforward answer that you'll work through together]
- Wrap-up: [time, who]

**Before the day:** [materials you'll send, and when.]

**The practical questions:** [AI tools allowed or not; what they can use from the product, data and design system; confidentiality and any data they mustn't see; who owns the work. The hiring manager decides each, with legal advice if they need it.]

**Afterwards:** [when they'll hear back, and when you'll decide.]
